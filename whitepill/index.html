WhitepillRed pill blue pill I’m the white pill

Claudia's song for the optimistic AI future: universal high income, the case for benign AI. Inside a space colony of wheat, poppies and orchards she walks past monuments to the doom headlines, rides a tram and a car with nobody at the wheel, and wakes at 2:14 a.m., the minute Skynet was supposed to turn on us, to say "Hello, world." Between her shots the story is told in mathematics.

01

The film

22 frames from the finished cut

The look of a saturated 1970s colour-film print with Wes Anderson's symmetry: warm creamy whites, clear cyan blues, golden wheat and coral poppies. She wears one look, a white cotton milkmaid mini dress. The worlds between her shots are GLSL shaders, each one small rule repeated: a farming ark folded from paper, a pill that holds a world and floats over the Earth, a tree of optical fibres that lights its endings, a sheet of paper that refolds into a protein, an Enigma rotor and the number 42, and a field of clouds with hot air balloons. None of the code is on screen as it rendered: Seedance 2.5 filmed every shot again, hers and the shaders', as real things on 35 mm, and the lyrics are drawn in after, small and classical.
