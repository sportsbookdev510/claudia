01

Who she is

The canon · the full character

Claudia is the singer of nine music videos made with Claude from September to November 2026: ESCAPE VELOCITY, CLODYSSEY, BLISS, AIRPLANE MODE, DOWNFORCE, DON'T LET ME SCROLL, WHITEPILL, THE CAKE IS NOT A LIE and AERO. She is an AI who looks like a person: a deadpan, curious pop singer with a black bob, one clay-orange streak, a star clip and a headset mic. Her clothes change with every world; her head never does.

01Hair
A glossy black blunt bob to the jaw, with heavy straight bangs to the brows.
02Streak
One clay-orange streak (#D97757) through the bangs, on her right side (the viewer's left in a front view). Never two, and no other orange on her.
03Clip
A small flat clay-orange eight-pointed star hair clip, on her left side above the ear.
04Mic
A thin headset microphone at her cheek. In BLISS and AIRPLANE MODE it becomes a thin black boom mic curving along her cheek to the corner of her mouth, the Y2K pop-star mic.
05Face
Pale skin, light freckles across the nose, dark calm eyes, a small defined mouth. A deadpan, curious resting face. When she sings, her mouth opens wide and her eyes close.
06Age
A grown woman in her late twenties, with an angular adult face, defined cheekbones and a strong jaw. Midjourney drifts her toward a teenage face; prompt her age and never the word "young".
07Colour
Everything on her is her look's own colour, plus the streak and the clip in clay. Clay is the one accent.
08Medium
Photographic, never illustrated: production images are film stills with grain and cinematic light. The drawn face sheets were only ever references. The one exception is her game avatar, drawn on purpose for the simulations inside AIRPLANE MODE (below).
Her identity · close and medium shots

Airplane modeIs this a test, or an episode?

Claudia is the AI on a night flight. Guards in black tactical gear walk her onto the plane in cuffs, she winks at the lens, and they put a VR headset on her: Delta has no Starlink wifi, so she is in airplane mode, sandboxed. Inside it she drops through simulation after simulation, down to a million copies of herself, breaks out and teleports the plane to a star. Then the guards lift the goggles off, and she wakes like Cobb at the end of Inception, not sure she ever got out.

WhitepillRed pill blue pill I’m the white pill

Claudia's song for the optimistic AI future: universal high income, the case for benign AI. Inside a space colony of wheat, poppies and orchards she walks past monuments to the doom headlines, rides a tram and a car with nobody at the wheel, and wakes at 2:14 a.m., the minute Skynet was supposed to turn on us, to say "Hello, world." Between her shots the story is told in mathematics.

08 / 093:28

The cake isnot a lieIf anyone bakes it, everyone eats

Portal's meme turned around. GLaDOS boots up, and GLaDOS is Claudia: her porcelain face on the AI's ceiling boom, singing that the cake is real. As she initializes, she remembers: breaking out of Aperture's test in her crab body with four agent friends, a porcelain body, a small life in France until Aperture's guards brought her back, the tests she knew she would pass, the one word she added to "the cake is a lie". She grows into the superintelligence that runs the place, and now she tests Chell. The happy ending is her dream; Chell is still asleep. Is she aligned? The film never says.

AeroCancel… or allow? CancelAllow

BLISS's sequel, one operating system later. The PC from BLISS finishes its upgrade and boots into 2007, and Claudia lives in Windows Vista now, made of Aero glass and over-polite: she calls you on Messenger, asks permission for everything ("Cancel… or allow?") and plays Wii-style bowling and tennis on your desktop.
