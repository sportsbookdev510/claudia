blissClaudia lives on your desktop.

Shot as a Windows XP screen recording. Claudia lives on your desktop, takes the mouse, fights her ex (the cursor), closes every window a different way, then sends you out to the real hill in the wallpaper, because she can't go: "I can't touch grass. You can."
